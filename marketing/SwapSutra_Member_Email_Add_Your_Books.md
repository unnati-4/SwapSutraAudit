## Email body

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for joining SwapSutra. There are now readers signing up from across the country — and that's exactly when the next part starts to matter.

Right now, most shelves on SwapSutra are still closed. Yours might be one of them.

A book sitting unread in your cupboard isn't doing anything. But the moment it's on your shelf here, it becomes visible to someone nearby who has been meaning to read exactly that book — and they probably have something on their shelf that you've been meaning to read.

**Two small things, and you're set up.**

### 1. Complete your profile

Add your name, your city, and a line or two about what you read.

Your city is the important one — swaps happen between readers who are close enough to actually meet. Without it, we can't show you the readers around you.

### 2. Add your books — just scan the ISBN

This is faster than it sounds.

- Open **List a Book**
- Tap **Scan ISBN**
- Point your camera at the barcode on the back of the book

The title, author and cover fill in automatically. About ten seconds per book. If your camera isn't cooperating, you can type the ISBN in by hand instead — it works exactly the same way.

For each book, you choose what you want to do with it: **swap, lend, rent or sell**. And anything you'd rather not part with — a signed copy, a gift, the one you re-read every year — simply don't list it. There's no obligation to open your whole shelf.

**A suggestion to start with: five books.**

Pick five you've finished and won't return to. That's enough to start showing up in other readers' searches, and enough to see what's out there near you.

[**Open your shelf →**]

See you on the shelves,
**Team SwapSutra**

## Sending notes

- **Personalise the first name.** A "Hi there" email from a small community reads as a mass mail, which is the one thing it shouldn't.
- **One CTA per email.** Both drafts have a single button repeated — don't add "join a reading circle" or the ambassador program here. Split attention kills the action.
- **Segment the follow-up.** Only send the second email to members with zero listings. Sending "your shelf is empty" to someone who listed 12 books is the fastest way to lose them.
- **Fill in [X] with the real number** in the follow-up. A true small number beats an invented big one — early members like seeing something genuinely early.
- **Best send time:** weekday evenings, 8–10pm IST, or Sunday morning. Adding books is a relaxed-hours activity, not a workday one.
