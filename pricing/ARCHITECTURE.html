Price Boundary Engine

A reader scans an ISBN, picks a format and a condition, and gets a range they may list within — not a number handed to them, and not a box they can type anything into. Three figures, kept apart on purpose: the allowed listing range, SwapSutra’s reference value, and the security deposit.

01

Four corrections

Each came out of building the thing rather than reading the spec, and each changes what gets shipped.

P25 / median / P75 does not survive small samples changed

The spec asked for percentiles and then gave a worked example that was min / median / max. On the Atomic Habits observations 350, 399, 425, 450, 499, P25/P75 gives ₹399–₹450 — a band less than half as wide as the ₹350–₹499 in the example.

Below n≈5 percentiles carry no information: at n=2 the type-7 P25 is exactly x₁ + 0.25(x₂−x₁), a formatted constant. There are nine standard quantile definitions, and R and SAS disagree on P25 of four numbers. Split conformal prediction needs n≥9 for 90% coverage — a theorem, not a tooling limit.

Replaced with: a robust centre in log space, shrunk toward a group prior. eBay, Airbnb and Mercari all reached the same answer independently — pool across similar items.

The deposit must not follow the user's chosen price changed

The spec set the 60% security deposit from the price the owner picks. But the deposit is paid by the other reader. An owner free to move it has every incentive to pick the top of the band and none not to.

"The user chooses" would collapse to "every owner chooses the maximum" for swap and lend within a week. The boundary caps the abuse; it does not remove the incentive.

Now: the chosen price governs SELL, where the market corrects an overpriced listing by nobody buying it. Deposits for SWAP / RENT / LEND come from the reference price. deposit_from_band() takes no user price as an argument at all.

A reader-entered MRP is a claim, not evidence changed

An MRP typed into the listing form used to be stored as an observation and fed straight into the price anchor. It carried a lower confidence — but confidence is a weight, not a gate, so the number still moved the band, and the band decides what the other reader deposits.

Plausibility bounds did not close this. A category range rejects the absurd; it says nothing about whether ₹900 is the MRP of this book.

Now four tiers. PHOTO_VERIFIED (an admin read the printed MRP off the reader’s own photo) · CATALOGUE_VERIFIED · PUBLISHER_SOURCED (a quote checked against a real page) · USER_PROVISIONAL. The first three anchor a price. The fourth is recorded, shown to the reader as provisional, and cannot move money. A Gemini extraction with no verifiable source page can only ever reach the fourth.

Unofficial copies: declarable, not priceable refused

The declaration option ships, and your reasoning for it is right — if the platform offers no honest option, unauthorised copies get declared as "publisher edition" and the whole price dataset rots.

What does not ship is market-price research producing a reference price and range for them. That would make SwapSutra a rate card for unauthorised copies, and under the 60% rule the platform would hold money calculated from it. They are excluded from pricing and from paid transactions because facilitating their sale or rental creates unnecessary copyright and platform-risk exposure. That is a risk decision for SwapSutra to take — the specific statutory position is a question for your own legal advice, not one this document is competent to settle.

Instead: no observations collected, no boundary computed, ineligible for SELL and RENT. A database CHECK constraint makes this structural rather than a matter of pipeline discipline.

02

MRP is the spine

You chose Google Books as the price source. It returns Play Books ebook prices, which are not print prices — so it is structurally wrong for this, not merely sparse. Its terms also forbid charging users for an app that surfaces its data, and you charge ₹49/month.

Every other route is closed or gated. What remains is better than any of them.

The consequence is a better design, not a compromise. Your SELL rule — 75–80% of MRP for As New, decreasing with condition — is a function of MRP and condition. It needs no market data at all, so it works on day one, before a single observation has been collected. Market observations become a correction to MRP rather than the primary signal.

03

How a band is computed

Two questions, deliberately separated. The first version of the engine merged them and produced an identical band for an as-new copy and a fair one — condition is the largest single driver of second-hand price, and the bug only surfaced by running it on a real example.

1 · anchorWhat is this edition's new price? MRP answers it; new-retail observations refine it, 50/50.
2 · bandThe published condition ladder, applied to the anchor. Width is policy, not sample spread.
3 · pullDirect used-copy evidence at the matching grade shrinks the reference toward itself.
4 · capSwapSutra caps used-book pricing at the printed MRP. A platform policy, not a legal rule.
What the reader seesWhat it isWho decides it
“You may list this book for ₹X–₹Y”The allowed listing range, from the condition ladder applied to the anchor.The seller chooses any figure inside it. The server checks.
“SwapSutra reference value: ₹Z”The engine’s deterministic valuation for non-sale transactions. Not a claim about market value.The engine only. Never the seller.
“Security deposit: ₹D”60% of the reference value.Derived from the reference value, never from the seller’s chosen listing price.

Everything runs in log space — book prices vary multiplicatively, so a ₹200 novel and a ₹2,000 textbook have no comparable additive spread. The prior is fitted on ln(price / MRP), the discount ratio, because ratios pool across price scales and rupees do not.

Estimator by sample size

nCentreOutlier filterWhy
0Prior only—Labelled as an estimate, never as a measurement.
1–2Geometric meannoneThe median of two points is their mean. No robustness available.
3–4MediannoneAn outlier and a small sample are indistinguishable here.
5–7MedianHampel, 3·MADNIST screens at 3, not Tukey's 1.5 — discarding a real observation costs more.
8+20% trimmed meanHampel, 3·MADEfficient under mild contamination, 20% breakdown point.

The failure mode that eats good data. MAD == 0 is not a corner case here — three sources all reporting ₹399 is the majority case for MRP observations, which are supposed to agree. An unguarded Hampel filter then gives every non-identical point an infinite score and deletes every genuine observation that differs at all. The scale is floored at 0.10 in log space, which is a fixed percentage in rupee space and so means the same thing for a ₹150 novel and a ₹4,000 textbook.

04

What Gemini does, and what it must not

Reads pages. Never computes.

Extracting "the printed MRP is ₹399, this is a paperback, this is the Indian edition" from unstructured page text is a language task, and an LLM is the right tool for it.

Computing a boundary, a median, a shrinkage weight or a deposit is not. Those numbers decide how much money one reader hands another.

Reproducibility — the same inputs must give the same band, and the model will be upgraded underneath you.
Auditability — when a reader is refused a price, someone must be able to say which observations produced the boundary and by which rule. "The model decided" is not an answer you can give a user, or a defence if the figure is disputed.
A provable floor — a test pins median([350,399,425]) == 399 forever. No test pins an LLM's arithmetic.

The hallucination gate

Asked what a book costs, a model produces a confident, plausible, invented number. The defence that actually works is never asking: the prompt supplies page text and demands a verbatim quote for every price, and a quote that is not a substring of the source is discarded automatically. Live output from extract.py:

Rejected extractions are stored with excluded = TRUE and their reason, never dropped. A filter you cannot inspect is a filter you cannot debug — and a spike in one rejection reason is the earliest warning that a source changed its page layout.

Row-level security lets the client read editions and boundaries and write nothing. book_prices, price_history and price_priors have RLS on and no policy at all, which makes them invisible to anon and authenticated entirely — raw observations are the input to a number that moves money, and no client needs them.

Migrating what you already have

08

Order of work

Phase one delivers a working boundary system with no market-price research at all. That is the point — every later phase improves an already-shipping product rather than being a precondition for one.

PhaseWhat shipsDepends on
1Supabase project, schema, legacy import, MRP + condition ladder. Bands live for every book with a known MRP.nothing
2ISBN capture in the listing form; user-declared MRP validated against category plausibility; server-side price validation wired into createBook.phase 1
3Open Library ingest. Catalogue autofills title, author, publisher, format from a scan.phase 1
4Gemini extraction against publisher sites only. Observations begin accumulating.phases 1–3
5Refit priors from real SwapSutra transactions. This is where the system starts being genuinely about the Indian used-book market rather than about MRP.~6 months of trades

Two things to check yourself before phase 4. One curl to the Google Books volumes endpoint with country=IN settles whether INR prices come back at all and at what hit rate — I could not complete that call. And your real Books API quota is in Google Cloud Console; the widely-repeated 1,000/day figure has no authoritative source.
