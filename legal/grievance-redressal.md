## What you can bring to us

- Content that is unlawful, obscene, defamatory or infringing
- A listing you believe is pirated, counterfeit or misrepresented
- Harassment, threats, abuse or discrimination by another reader
- Impersonation of you or someone else
- A privacy concern, or a request to access, correct or delete your data
- A safety incident during or after an exchange
- A membership billing problem
- A deposit that has not been returned
- Anything else that has gone wrong

## What to include

To help us act quickly, please tell us:

1. **Your name and registered email address**
2. **What happened**, as specifically as you can
3. **Where** — a listing, a chat, a profile, a page
4. **When** — dates and approximate times
5. **What you would like us to do**
6. **Anything supporting it** — screenshots, photographs, message references

Send it to **[GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** with a clear subject line.

> **Anonymous complaints.** We will always act on a safety concern, however it reaches us. But we cannot report an outcome back to someone we cannot contact, and we may be unable to investigate fully without details.

## Urgent matters

**Non-consensual intimate imagery.** Under Rule 3(2)(b), we remove such content within **24 hours** of a complaint. Email **[GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** with the subject line **"URGENT — Rule 3(2)(b)"**.

**Immediate danger.** If you or someone else is in danger, **call 112 first.** We are not an emergency service. Tell us afterwards and we will act on the account and preserve the record.

**Cybercrime.** You can also report at **https://cybercrime.gov.in** or on **1930**.

## What we can and cannot do

We think you deserve to know this before you write to us, rather than after.

**We can:** remove content, restrict or terminate accounts, mediate between readers using the record we hold, correct or delete your data, refund a membership fee we charged in error, and give you the complete exchange record to use elsewhere.

**We cannot:** compel a reader to return a book or a deposit, recover money on your behalf, reimburse you for another reader's conduct, or act as a court. SwapSutra never holds the money exchanged between readers, so we cannot return it.

Where a reader repeatedly fails to honour their commitments, removing them from the community is the strongest tool we have — and we use it.

## If you are not satisfied

**For data protection matters**, you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

**For consumer matters**, you may approach a **Consumer Commission** under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — including the one where you reside. The **National Consumer Helpline** is on **1915**, or at **https://consumerhelpline.gov.in**.

**For content matters** under the IT Rules, 2021, you may appeal to a **Grievance Appellate Committee** at **https://gac.gov.in** within 30 days of our decision.

Nothing here limits your right to any other legal remedy.
