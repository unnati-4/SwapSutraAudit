## 1. This is a volunteer role

This is **unpaid**. The Ambassador is not an employee, contractor, agent, or
franchisee of SwapSutra, and nothing here creates that relationship. In
exchange for their time, SwapSutra offers:

- practical experience in community-building and outreach;
- a certificate of participation on completion (template attached), describing
  the specific work reviewed and confirmed under clause 3A; and
- [ANY OTHER BENEFIT — e.g. free membership, merchandise, or "none"].

**No cash payment, commission, or per-signup incentive is offered under this
role.** If that ever changes, a new agreement is needed before it starts.

## 2. What the role involves

The Ambassador may, at their own pace and by their own choice:

- tell fellow students about SwapSutra;
- share official SwapSutra content (provided by SwapSutra) on their own social
  media or campus channels;
- help organise or promote a SwapSutra event or book drive on campus, with
  SwapSutra's involvement;
- collect informal feedback from students and pass it to SwapSutra.

**There are no fixed hours, no minimum signups, and no targets.** The
Ambassador may do as much or as little as they choose, and may stop at any
time by telling SwapSutra.

## 3A. Submitting proof of work

**Whatever the Ambassador does under this role must be submitted to the
admin, Unnati Goyal, along with proof**, so it can be reviewed and — if
confirmed — reflected on the certificate of participation.

Proof means something simple and verifiable for the activity in question:
a screenshot of a shared post, a photo from an event, a short note on
feedback collected, a copy of a flyer distributed, or similar. There is no
fixed format and no minimum amount — the point is that the certificate
describes work that was actually seen and confirmed, not work claimed but
never shown.

**How to submit:** email it to swapsutra@gmail.com with the Ambassador's
name and college in the subject line, referencing this role. (SwapSutra.in
is the site — swapsutra@gmail.com is the address that actually receives
mail; use the email for anything you need to send.)

Only activity submitted and confirmed this way will be named in the
Ambassador's certificate. Activity that was never submitted, or that the
admin could not verify, will not be described as completed.

## 3B. What the Ambassador may not do

- **Speak for SwapSutra on anything official.** No promises about pricing,
  refunds, membership terms, partnerships, or anything not already published
  on SwapSutra.in.
- **Collect money on SwapSutra's behalf.** Membership fees and any in-app
  payments go through the app itself, never through an Ambassador.
- **Represent this as paid work, an internship, or employment** to their
  college, a placement cell, or anyone else. It is a volunteer role.
- **Use the SwapSutra name, logo or branding** other than the official
  material SwapSutra provides, without asking first.
- **Share user data.** An Ambassador does not get access to SwapSutra's user
  database, admin tools, or any reader's personal information. If a student
  mentions a problem, the Ambassador's job is to point them to
  swapsutra@gmail.com — not to try to fix it themselves.

## 4. Conduct

The Ambassador agrees to represent SwapSutra courteously and honestly, and to
follow SwapSutra's [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines) in anything
they say on SwapSutra's behalf. SwapSutra may end this role at any time,
without needing to give a reason, if conduct falls short of this.

## 5. Confidentiality

Anything SwapSutra shares with the Ambassador that is clearly internal
(unreleased plans, drafts, non-public numbers) stays confidential, both
during and after the role.

## 6. Ending the role

Either side may end this at any time, for any reason, with a simple message —
no notice period, no penalty. A certificate (see attached template) is issued
reflecting whatever period the Ambassador actually participated.

## 7. No warranty, no liability beyond what's fair

This is a volunteer arrangement between two parties acting in good faith.
SwapSutra is not liable for any cost, loss or inconvenience the Ambassador
incurs while volunteering (e.g. travel, printing, time), unless SwapSutra
specifically agreed in writing to cover it in advance. The Ambassador is not
liable for outcomes outside their control (e.g. how many students actually
sign up).

## 8. Governing law

This is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute is handled informally
first — write to swapsutra@gmail.com — before anything more formal.
