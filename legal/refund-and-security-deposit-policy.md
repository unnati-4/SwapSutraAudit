# Part A — SwapSutra's fees

Joining SwapSutra is free and membership does not expire. SwapSutra charges two fees, both paid by UPI and verified by hand, usually within a day.

## A1. Platform fee — ₹10 per reader, per exchange

Charged to each reader when an exchange is accepted, before the exchange chat opens. Where the exchange also needs a security deposit, the QR code shows the two together; they are recorded separately, and only the deposit is ever refundable under Part B.

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| The exchange is cancelled before both readers' payments are verified and the chat opens | **Full refund** |
| You were charged twice, or paid the wrong amount | **Refund of the excess** |
| The chat has opened | **No refund** |
| Your account is suspended for breach of our Terms or Community Guidelines | **No refund** |

## A2. Listing unlock — ₹20, one time

Lets you list more than 20 books. Never expires.

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| We could not verify your payment | **Full refund** |
| You were charged twice | **Refund of the duplicate** |
| You ask within **48 hours** and have not listed a 21st book | **Full refund** |
| Otherwise | **No refund** |

## A3. How to request a refund

Email **swapsutra@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Refund request"**, including your registered email, the payment reference (UTR), the date and amount, and what happened.

- We acknowledge within **48 hours**.
- We decide within **7 working days**.
- Approved refunds are returned to the **original payment method** within **7–10 working days**.

If you disagree with the outcome, escalate to our Grievance Officer — see the [Grievance Redressal Policy](/grievance).

# Part B — Security deposits

## B1. What a deposit is

When a book is lent temporarily, the borrower puts up a refundable deposit of
**up to 60% of the book's printed MRP**. It exists only to make the owner whole
if the book does not come back, and it must be returned in full when it does.

A deposit is **security, not a fee**.

> ### ⚠️ Interim arrangement — please read
>
> Deposits are currently collected and released **manually by SwapSutra** while we
> complete our transition to a regulated escrow facility with an authorised payment
> aggregator. Until that transition is complete, a deposit is **not** held in escrow,
> and SwapSutra cannot guarantee it against events affecting the receiving account.
>
> If you would prefer not to place a deposit under this interim arrangement, please do
> not use Temporary exchanges. We will update this page the moment escrow is live.

## B2. How much may be asked for

A deposit may be **no more than 60% of the book's printed MRP**. This is a community rule, and asking for more is a breach of our Terms.

For a book with no printed MRP, readers should agree a reasonable figure based on current retail value. If you cannot agree, do not proceed with the exchange.

## B3. The deposit is returned in full when

- the book is returned within the agreed period, in substantially the condition it went out in (ordinary reading wear is expected and is not damage); **or**
- the exchange is cancelled before the book changes hands; **or**
- the owner fails to hand over the book at all.

**Timeline: within 48 hours of the return being confirmed in the app by both readers.**

## B4. Part of the deposit may be retained only where

| Situation | What may be retained |
|---|---|
| The book is not returned at all | Up to the full deposit |
| The book is returned significantly damaged beyond ordinary reading wear | A fair amount reflecting the damage — not automatically the whole deposit |
| The book is returned very late, after reasonable reminders | A fair amount, agreed in advance if possible |

**Ordinary reading wear is not damage.** A softened spine, a slightly bumped corner, or the ordinary marks of a book having been read are exactly what a book-sharing community is for. Retaining a deposit over ordinary wear is a breach of our Terms, and we will act on it.

**A reason must be stated in writing in the exchange chat** before any part of a deposit is retained, and the borrower may dispute it under B5. Silent retention is a breach.

## B5. If a deposit is not returned

1. **Ask in the exchange chat.** Most cases are a delay, not a refusal.
2. **Raise it with us within 14 days** of the return being confirmed — in the chat or at swapsutra@gmail.com.
3. **We review the record**: what was agreed, the condition proof on both sides, the handover confirmations, and the messages. We give a reasoned view within **7 working days**.
4. **We release the deposit** in line with that view, or explain why we have not.
5. **We act on the account.** A reader who withholds or disputes a deposit unfairly will have their account restricted, and removed if it happens again.

## B6. Rent and purchase payments

The same principle applies. Rent and purchase money is paid **directly between readers**. SwapSutra does not collect, hold or refund it. Terms are agreed between the two readers before the exchange, and recorded in the app.

## B7. Delivery and courier costs

Where a book travels by courier, the readers agree who pays. Courier charges are **never refundable by SwapSutra**, and a courier's loss or damage is a matter between the sender and the courier. See the [Exchange & Delivery Policy](/exchange-policy).
