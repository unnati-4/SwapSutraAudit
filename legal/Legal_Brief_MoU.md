# Brief for legal advisor — SwapSutra × [Partner Store] MoU

**Send this only after the 30-day pilot has run and both sides want to continue.**
Do not commission this before the first conversation. A lawyer-drafted document
arriving at the start of a relationship with a small independent store reads as
disproportionate and usually kills the deal. The business proposal comes first;
this is the paperwork that follows a working relationship.

---

## 1. What I need

A short **Memorandum of Understanding** — two to three pages, plain English,
no schedules unless strictly necessary. It should be readable by a small
shop owner without a lawyer present. This is deliberately **not** meant to be
a binding commercial contract; it records what each side has agreed to do.

Please flag anywhere you think a non-binding MoU is the wrong instrument.

SwapSutra is an online platform on which readers list physical books and

**Party B** — [Legal name of the store and its proprietor], a pre-owned

## 3. What each party is agreeing to do

**Party B (the store) will:**
- Allow its premises to be listed on the SwapSutra platform as a designated
  "Exchange Point", where two SwapSutra users may meet to hand over books.
- Make its premises available for a book-swap gathering once a month, for
  approximately two hours, at a mutually agreed time.
- Permit a small shelf or table on its premises to hold books in circulation.
- List a minimum of 25 of its own titles on the SwapSutra platform.

**Party A (SwapSutra) will:**
- Name and link the store on swapsutra.in as a Founding Partner.
- Provide the store lifetime free access to the platform, and charge it no
  commission, listing fee or subscription.
- Organise, publicise, staff and clean up after each monthly gathering.
- Refer users who wish to sell books for cash to the store.

## 4. Terms that must appear

1. **No consideration.** No money passes in either direction. No fee,
   commission, revenue share or joining amount.
2. **No equity, no partnership in law.** The two businesses remain separate
   and independently owned. Please make clear this does **not** create a
   partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the
   parties, and neither may bind the other.
3. **No exclusivity.** Either party may work with anyone else, in any capacity.
4. **Termination.** Either party may end the arrangement on seven days'
   written notice, without cause and without penalty.
5. **Liability — this is the clause that matters most to me.** The store is
   providing a meeting place only. It must carry **no** liability for:
   the condition of any book exchanged; any dispute, loss or damage arising
   between two SwapSutra users; or the conduct of any user on its premises
   beyond its ordinary duties as an occupier. Disputes between users are
   handled through SwapSutra's own process. Please draft this so the store
   owner can see plainly that she is not taking on risk — that is the single
   thing most likely to stop her signing.
6. **Data.** Each party's customer and user data remains its own. Neither
   discloses personal data of its customers or users to the other. Please
   confirm whether anything further is needed here under the Digital Personal
   Data Protection Act, 2023.
7. **Intellectual property.** Each party may use the other's name and logo
   solely to describe this arrangement, and must stop on termination. Neither
   acquires any other right in the other's marks.
8. **Term.** Initial term of six months, continuing thereafter unless ended
   under the termination clause.
9. **Governing law and forum.** Indian law. Please advise on the sensible
   forum given Party A is in Uttar Pradesh and Party B in Haryana, and
   whether a mediation-first clause is worth including at this size.

## 5. Design and branding

The document should carry both logos at the top, side by side.
SwapSutra's logo is attached. The store's logo will be supplied.
Please keep the visual treatment restrained — this should read as a serious
but friendly document, not a commercial contract.
