**We are not a party to any exchange between readers.** When you swap, lend, rent, borrow, buy or sell a book through SwapSutra:

- we make no promise about any book's condition, authenticity, ownership, or fitness; and
- we make no promise about any reader's conduct, honesty, reliability or identity.

We operate as an **intermediary** within the meaning of Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000. We do not initiate transmissions, select their receivers, or modify the information in them.

**What we do offer:** listing review, condition and handover proof tools, a record of what was agreed, a messaging channel, community standards we enforce, and a good-faith dispute mediation process (clause 15). **One exception:** security deposits on temporary exchanges are held and released by SwapSutra under clause 8 and our [Refund & Security Deposit Policy](/refund-policy). That is the single point at which we handle money moving between readers, and it is described in full there.

## 7. Exchanges between readers

SwapSutra supports four kinds of exchange:

| Type | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Swap** | Two readers exchange books, temporarily or permanently. |
| **Lend / Borrow** | One reader lends a book to another for an agreed period, at no charge. |
| **Rent** | One reader lends a book for an agreed period for an agreed fee. |
| **Sell / Buy** | One reader sells a book to another. |

For each exchange, the two readers agree the terms between themselves: duration, condition expectations, any fee, any deposit, and how the book will travel. **SwapSutra records what was agreed; it does not set or guarantee those terms.**

Logistics and delivery are governed by our [Exchange & Delivery Policy](/exchange-policy).
