## 1. The short version

SwapSutra connects readers. **Readers move the books themselves.** How a book travels — in person, by courier, or otherwise — is agreed between the two readers, arranged by them, and paid for by them. SwapSutra is not a courier, a warehouse, or an insurer, and we never take possession of a book.

---

## 2. Agreeing the exchange

Before a book moves, both readers agree, in the app:

- **The type of exchange** — swap, lend, rent or sell
- **Duration**, for anything temporary
- **Any money** — rent, purchase price, security deposit
- **How the book will travel** — in person or by courier
- **Who pays for delivery**, if it is travelling
- **When and where** the handover happens

Once both readers confirm, that record is what we rely on if anything goes wrong. **Agree it properly, in the app, before the book moves.** An arrangement made off-platform is one we cannot help you with.

---

## 3. Meeting in person

For neighbourhood exchanges, meeting in person is the heart of what SwapSutra is for.

**Meet somewhere public and well-lit** — a café, a bookshop, a library, a busy market. We strongly discourage private residences, especially for a first exchange.

Before you go:

- Tell someone you trust where you are going and when
- Keep the conversation in the app, so there is a record
- Arrive on time, or tell the other reader you are running late

At the handover:

- **Inspect the book** before completing the exchange
- **Take condition photographs** and upload them — both readers, both directions
- **Confirm the handover in the app** immediately

If you feel uncomfortable at any point, **leave**. No book is worth it. Tell us afterwards at swapsutra@gmail.com.

> **We do not run background checks on readers.** Verification confirms an email address and, where given, a phone number. It is not an identity check or a character reference. Your safety is your own responsibility.

## 4. Condition proof

Condition photographs are the single most useful thing you can do to protect yourself, and they are required for every temporary exchange.

**At handover, both readers photograph:** the cover, the spine, the edges, and any existing damage.
**At return, both readers photograph the same things again.**

These photographs are visible to both readers in the exchange, and to our team if a dispute is raised. Without them, a disagreement about condition becomes one person's word against another's, and there is very little we can do.

## 5. Sending a book by courier

For exchanges outside your neighbourhood, readers arrange and pay for delivery themselves — Borzo, Porter, Blue Dart, India Post, Delhivery, or any service you both agree on.

**Agree in advance who pays.** Common practice is that the sender pays outbound and the borrower pays the return, but you are free to agree anything.

If you are sending a book:

- **Pack it properly** — bubble wrap or a padded envelope, not a thin plastic bag
- **Photograph it packed and sealed**, and share the photograph in the chat
- **Share the tracking number** in the app
- **Consider insuring** anything valuable or irreplaceable

**SwapSutra is not responsible for transit.** Delays, loss, theft or damage in the hands of a courier are between the sender and the courier company. Courier costs are never refundable by SwapSutra.

## 6. Returning a borrowed book

- **Return on or before the agreed date.** If you need more time, ask — most readers will say yes if you ask before the date, and very few will if you ask after.
- **Return it in the condition you received it**, allowing for ordinary reading wear.
- **Photograph it** and upload the return condition proof.
- **Confirm the return in the app**, both of you.

Once the owner confirms receipt, any **security deposit must be returned within 48 hours** — see the [Refund & Security Deposit Policy](/refund-policy).

## 7. Ordinary wear versus damage

This distinction matters, so we will be explicit about it.

**Ordinary reading wear — expected, and not damage:**
a softened or creased spine · slightly bumped corners · minor shelf wear · a faint fingerprint · the ordinary marks of a book having been read by someone

**Damage — a fair basis for a deposit deduction:**
torn or missing pages · water or liquid damage · a broken binding · significant staining · writing, highlighting or annotation that was not there before · a lost book

SwapSutra is a community for books that are meant to be read, not preserved under glass. **A book that comes back looking read has been used exactly as intended.** If you would be upset by that, please do not lend that particular copy.

## 8. When things go wrong

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The book has not arrived** | Check the tracking, message the sender, give it 48 hours. Then raise it with us. |
| **The book arrived damaged in transit** | Photograph it before opening it fully. Raise it with the courier — that is your claim. Tell the other reader and us. |
| **The book is not as described** | Raise it in the chat within **48 hours** of receipt, with photographs. Do not wait. |
| **The book has not been returned** | Message the borrower. If there is no response for 7 days after the due date, raise it with us. |
| **The deposit has not come back** | See §5 of the [Refund & Security Deposit Policy](/refund-policy). |
| **Something happened at a meeting** | If you are in danger, **call 112**. Then tell us at swapsutra@gmail.com. |

**Raise disputes within 14 days.** After that the record gets cold and we can do much less with it.

## 9. What SwapSutra does and does not do

**We do:** review listings before they go live, hold condition proof and handover records, give you a messaging channel with admin oversight, mediate in good faith using the record, and restrict or remove readers who do not honour their commitments.

**We do not:** take possession of books, ship anything, insure anything, guarantee any book's condition or authenticity, hold or refund money between readers, or compel any reader to do anything.

Our full position is set out in clause 4 of the [Terms of Use](/terms).
