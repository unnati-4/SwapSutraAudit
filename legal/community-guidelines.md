# SwapSutra — Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** [DD Month 2026]

> These guidelines form part of the [Terms of Use](/terms). They exist partly
> because Rule 3(1)(a) of the IT Rules, 2021 requires every intermediary to
> publish rules telling users what may not be shared — and mostly because a
> community works better when the standards are written down.

---

## The idea

SwapSutra exists so that books keep moving between people who will actually read them. Everything below follows from that.

**Be honest about your books. Be kind to other readers. Bring the book back.**

---

## 1. Honest listings

**List only books you physically own.** Not a friend's, not one you have borrowed, not one you are hoping to acquire.

**Describe the condition truthfully.** New, Gently Used, Well Loved — and mention the tear, the water stain, the missing dust jacket. A reader who knows what they are getting is almost never disappointed. A reader who does not, always is.

**Use real photographs of your actual copy.** Not stock images, not the publisher's cover shot.

**Only genuine copies.** Pirated, photocopied, scanned and counterfeit books are prohibited without exception. Lending, renting or selling a book you legally bought is lawful. Circulating a reproduction is not, and it takes money from the writers we are all here for.

Every listing is reviewed by a curator before it goes live.

---

## 2. Honest exchanges

**Turn up.** If you cannot make it, say so as early as you can.

**Return on time.** If you need longer, ask before the date, not after.

**Return the deposit promptly** — within 48 hours of the book coming back.

**Do not withhold a deposit over ordinary reading wear.** A read book looks read. That is the point.

**Keep it on the platform.** Agreeing terms in the app is what lets us help if something goes wrong. An arrangement made entirely over WhatsApp is one we have no record of.

---

## 3. How we treat each other

**Expected:** courtesy, patience, and the assumption that the other person means well. Most problems on SwapSutra are a delay, not a betrayal.

**Not tolerated:**

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or stalking
- Abuse or discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, language, region or anything else
- Unwanted romantic or sexual advances — SwapSutra is not a dating platform
- Sharing another reader's contact details, photographs or address without consent
- Impersonating another person or a SwapSutra administrator
- Repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop

---

## 4. What may not be posted

You may not post, list, upload or share anything that:

- is obscene, pornographic, or paedophilic
- is defamatory, or invades another person's privacy
- is hateful, or incites violence or discrimination
- is knowingly false or misleading
- infringes anyone's copyright, trademark or other rights
- impersonates another person
- threatens the unity, integrity, defence, security or sovereignty of India, or public order
- is malicious code, or is designed to interfere with SwapSutra
- promotes an unrelated commercial product or service
- is spam

*(This reflects Rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules, 2021.)*

---

## 5. Reviews and ratings

**Review the exchange, not the person.** Was the book as described? Did they turn up? Was it returned in good order?

- Be specific and fair
- Do not use reviews to threaten or pressure another reader
- Do not review yourself, your friends, or an exchange that never happened
- Do not include anyone's personal details

We remove reviews that are abusive, fabricated, or unrelated to an actual exchange.

---

## 6. Reading circles and meetups

Circles and events are for readers. Bring curiosity, let others finish their sentences, and expect people to have read the same book differently from you.

Spoilers: mark them. Promotion: don't. Recording a session: ask everyone first.

Hosts may set additional house rules, and may remove someone who ignores them.

---

## 7. Your safety

**Meet in public places.** Tell someone where you are going. Trust your instincts and leave if something feels wrong.

**Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card number, CVV or bank password** — not with another reader, and not with anyone claiming to be from SwapSutra. **We will never ask you for them.**

If you are in danger, **call 112**. Then tell us.

---

## 8. What happens if you break these rules

We aim to be proportionate. Most first problems are a misunderstanding, and a conversation fixes them.

| Level | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1. A word** | We explain what went wrong and ask you to put it right |
| **2. Content removal** | A listing, review or message comes down |
| **3. Restriction** | You keep your account but lose some features for a period |
| **4. Suspension** | Your account is paused while we investigate |
| **5. Removal** | Your account is closed permanently |

**We skip straight to suspension or removal** where there is a threat to someone's safety, harassment, non-consensual imagery, fraud, impersonation, systematic listing of pirated books, or a repeat of something we have already warned you about.

**Deposits and books outlast accounts.** Losing your account does not release you from returning a book or refunding a deposit.

**If you think we got it wrong,** write to our [Grievance Officer](/grievance). We will look again and reply within 15 days.

---

## 9. Telling us about a problem

Report it in the app, or write to **swapsutra@gmail.com**. For anything formal or urgent, go to our [Grievance Officer](/grievance) — acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 15 days.

Please include what happened, where, when, and any screenshots.

**We would genuinely rather know.** A community only stays good if the people in it tell us when it stops being good.

---

*Be honest about your books. Be kind to other readers. Bring the book back.* 📚
