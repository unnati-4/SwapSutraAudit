## 2. You must be 18

SwapSutra is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect the personal data of children. If we discover an account belongs to someone under 18, we suspend it and delete the data.

If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us at **swapsutra@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 When you register

| Data | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Name** | So other readers know who they are meeting |
| **Email address** | To sign you in, and to send you exchange notifications |
| **Phone number** | So a matched reader can coordinate a handover |
| **Area and pincode** | To show you books near you — this is the core of the service |
| **Date of birth** | To confirm you are 18 or over |

### 3.2 What you choose to add

Reading genres, a short bio, a profile photograph, and your notification preferences. All optional. All editable or removable at any time.

### 3.3 When you list a book

Title, author, ISBN, edition, condition, your description, photographs of the book, and the **approximate location** of the book so nearby readers can find it.

> **Location.** Book listings carry latitude and longitude. **Precise coordinates are never shown to logged-out visitors**, and are revealed only to members. We recommend you set a location that represents your neighbourhood rather than your doorstep.

### 3.4 When you exchange a book

The exchange record: what was agreed, condition photographs uploaded by both readers, handover confirmations, timestamps, and any deposit amount recorded.

### 3.5 When you talk to other readers

Messages, and voice notes if you record them. **We can read exchange messages.** We do this for safety and to mediate disputes — an admin is a visible participant in exchange conversations, and the app tells you so. It is not private correspondence, and it is not end-to-end encrypted.

### 3.6 Your reading life

Your bookshelf, TBR list, reading progress, favourites, reading log, streaks, and reading-circle participation.

### 3.7 Membership payments

Your payment reference (UTR), the amount, the date, and any payment screenshot you upload. **We never see or store your UPI PIN, card number, CVV, or bank credentials** — those never reach us.

### 3.8 Automatically

Your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from your IP. Used for security, abuse prevention, and understanding what is working.

### 3.9 What we never collect

We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN, bank account numbers, biometrics, health data, caste, religion, or political affiliation. We have no reason to, and we do not want them.

## 4. Why we process it, and on what basis

Under the DPDP Act we process personal data on the basis of **your consent**, given when you register and when you use a specific feature, and for **certain legitimate uses** permitted by the Act.

| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and running your account | Consent |
| Showing you books near you | Consent |
| Connecting you with a matched reader | Consent |
| Recording an exchange and its condition proof | Consent |
| Sending exchange and community notifications | Consent |
| **Sending your text to Google's Gemini API when you use the Quill assistant** | **Consent — you choose to use the feature** |
| Reviewing listings for quality and authenticity | Consent |
| Mediating a dispute | Consent + legitimate use |
| Preventing fraud, abuse and impersonation | Legitimate use |
| Keeping records we are required to keep by law | Legal obligation |

**We do not** use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers.

## 6. Who else touches your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each is a Data Processor acting on our instructions; we remain responsible for your data.

| Provider | What they handle | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Google** (Sheets, Apps Script, Drive) | Our database and backend. Everything in §3 is stored here. | Global |
| **Google Gemini API** | **Text you send to the Quill assistant.** Sent only when you use the feature. | Global |
| **Vercel** | Hosting and delivery of the website | Global |
| **[EMAIL PROVIDER]** | Sending you notification and OTP emails | [REGION] |

**About Quill and Gemini.** When you use the Quill assistant, the text of your prompt is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Do not put anything into Quill that you would not want processed by a third party. If you would rather not use it, simply do not.

**WhatsApp.** We link to a public WhatsApp community. If you join it, you are in WhatsApp's hands, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy, and other members will see your WhatsApp number. Joining is entirely optional and separate from your SwapSutra account.

**Others.** We share data with law enforcement or a court where legally required, and with professional advisers under confidentiality. If SwapSutra is ever sold or merged, your data may transfer — we will tell you before that happens.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | While your account is active |
| After you delete your account | Deleted within **30 days**, except below |
| Exchange records and condition proof | **3 years** after the exchange — needed if a dispute surfaces later |
| Exchange messages | **1 year** after the exchange closes |
| Voice notes | **6 months** |
| Payment records | **8 years** — required under Indian tax and accounting law |
| Security and access logs | **1 year** |
| Anonymised, non-identifying statistics | Indefinitely |

When a retention period ends, we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it.

## 8. How we protect it

- Sign-in is by **one-time password** — there is no password for anyone to steal.
- Sessions are signed and can be revoked centrally.
- Access to our database is restricted to named administrators and reviewed periodically.
- Contact details are gated behind mutual consent.
- Precise book locations are gated behind membership.
- Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

**No system is perfectly secure.** We are a small team, our database is a Google Sheet, and we would rather tell you that plainly than imply a level of security we do not have. Please do not put anything into SwapSutra that would seriously harm you if it were exposed.

**If a breach happens**, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay — what happened, what data was involved, what it might mean for you, what we have done, and what you should do.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- **Know** what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate, and complete anything incomplete.
- **Erase** your data, where we no longer need it for the purpose you gave it for or to meet a legal obligation.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time — as easily as you gave it. Some features will stop working.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Complain** to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

**How to exercise them:** most things you can change yourself in your profile. For anything else, write to **swapsutra@gmail.com** from your registered email address. We respond within **90 days**, and usually much sooner.

**Deleting your account.** You can close your account from your profile at any time. Before you do, please settle any open exchange — return a borrowed book, refund a deposit. Deleting an account does not release you from what you owe another reader.
